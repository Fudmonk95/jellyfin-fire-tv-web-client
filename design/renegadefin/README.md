# RenegadeFin identity

An asymmetric flame curves into a fin; a cut-out play symbol links it to media. The detached spark gives the silhouette a rebellious, broken-boundary character. Orange #FF8735 and amber #FFB849 sit on charcoal #101114. This is original vector artwork, not the Jellyfin logo with a recolour.

`logo.svg` is the scalable mark. `banner.svg` is the 640×360 TV launcher tile. The icon's safe inset and banner text avoid stretching a phone icon into a wide TV slot. Both source vectors remain editable.

Native UI direction: featured artwork, horizontal shelves, limited animation, visible selected-card captions and warm focus fills. No animated background loops or autoplay trailers on entry-level Fire TV devices.

Inspiration reviewed: https://jellywatch.app/player — native browsing, profiles and server-enforced user policies. This does not imply access to Jellywatch source or a partnership.
